## Maximum intended voltage and magnetometer materials

The stated battery range is 3.0–4.2 V. TPS63802 accepts 1.3–5.5 V, MCP1700 accepts 2.3–6.0 V, and NCV8705 sees regulated ≈3.8 V against a 5.5 V operating maximum; these ratings cover the intended battery and regulator rails. For ordinary USB 5 V operation, use **5.25 V** as the upper check point: [ST USBLC6-2](../../datasheets/DS_usblc6-2.pdf) specifies leakage at VBUS = 5.25 V and VBUS breakdown ≥6 V; [AP2112](../../datasheets/AP2112.pdf) allows VIN to 6.0 V in operation and has a 6.5 V absolute maximum. The USB-input C21 is 16 V. This does not validate arbitrary USB overvoltage, surge, or a noncompliant supply; the ESD device is not a sustained overvoltage disconnect.

The BMM350 vicinity contains C12, C13, C14 and nearby power/routing structures. C12's Samsung part is an already accepted **nonmagnetic-certification exception** in the design document. Neither this BOM nor the local datasheets certify the complete nearby assembly as nonmagnetic. The practical acceptance test is powered, assembled-board hard-/soft-iron calibration and comparison across the MAX and ZED population variants; retain this as a prototype validation requirement rather than claiming a material qualification.

**Checklist disposition:** Keep the combined passive-rating item open until exact-part DC-bias minima are recorded for C1/C2, C4/C17, C18/C19, C21 and C25, and the MAX/ZED antenna-short resistor and choke cases are validated or explicitly accepted with a justified fault duration/thermal limit. The selected inductor and ordinary voltage ratings pass the paper review; C12's magnetic-material exception requires installed-board validation.
