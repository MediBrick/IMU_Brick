# Activity Brick V2 pin and pad audit — 2026-09-21

Scope: the saved schematic, saved PCB, and every project-local symbol and footprint used by them. Manufacturer pin tables and package/land-pattern drawings were compared directly; the 3D models were not used as pinout evidence. This is a CAD verification, not a physical assembly inspection.

The KiCad XML netlist was compared with PCB pad numbers for every used custom symbol. Every numbered schematic pin has a corresponding numbered PCB pad. All 17 used instances of project-local footprints have the same pad numbers, locations, sizes, and drills as their library masters. The manufacturer comparisons are below. `datasheets/` paths are relative to the project root.

Their [blue](https://www.xinglight.cn/uploadfile/202509/74e1407ba4890a4.pdf), [yellow](https://www.xinglight.cn/uploadfile/202506/529f892261aa8ba.pdf), and [green](https://www.xinglight.cn/uploadfile/202509/9bb79c5aba6539b.pdf) package/polarity drawings identify terminal 1 as cathode (−) and terminal 2 as anode (+). The PCB and schematic use that mapping on all five LEDs. Although the shared KiCad footprint name includes `Red`, the pad geometry and polarity match these selected parts.

No pin-number, polarity, mating-side, NC, or exposed-pad discrepancy was found in the saved CAD. The ZED central ground-pad numbering and NCV8705 split-pad representation are deliberate CAD conventions; they should be conveyed to assembly rather than interpreted as extra manufacturer pins. The separate solder-mask/paste and mechanical-fit checklist items remain open.
