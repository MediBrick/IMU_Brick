## PCBWay capability comparison (checked 2026-09-21)

PCBWay's [standard capability table](https://www.pcbway.com/capabilities.html) lists 0.10 mm minimum trace/space, 0.15 mm minimum annular ring for a via pad, and a 4 mil (0.1016 mm) standard green solder-mask bridge for copper below 2 oz. The BMM350 custom rule is 0.15 mm trace width and 0.15 mm copper clearance; these copper features fit the published standard trace/space limits. Its 0.10 mm nominal mask web is just below the 4 mil bridge figure. Its 0.15 mm copper gap between adjacent 0.25 mm lands is also below the 0.20 mm IC pad spacing that PCBWay's [mask-bridge help](https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/soldermask_issues/Soldermask_bridge.html) says is needed to retain a green mask bridge. The [advanced table](https://www.pcbway.com/advanced-pcb-capabilities.html) lists a 3 mil minimum mask bridge, but does not by itself guarantee nine separate openings on this exact 0.40 mm BGA pitch. Request PCBWay's CAM acceptance of the actual Gerbers, explicitly requiring separate NSMD openings or an approved alternate; do not assume the Advanced PCB selector alone resolves it.

The revised NCV8705 layout uses ordinary GND vias outside the component footprint and solder apertures. Their 0.30 mm drills and 0.60 mm copper diameters give 0.15 mm nominal annular rings, matching PCBWay's published standard minimum. The two previous plated holes within pad 7 are absent from both the saved board footprint and the project library. No [PCBWay via-in-pad](https://www.pcbway.com/pcb_prototype/PCB_Via_in_Pad.html) fill/cap process is needed for these two external vias. This conclusion assumes the final GND copper connection survives zone refill and DRC, and does not replace first-article solder-joint inspection.
