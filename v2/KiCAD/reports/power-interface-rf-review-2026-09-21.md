# Activity Brick V2 power, interface, and RF review — 2026-09-21

This is a source-level and saved-PCB review of the six production-release checks under “Power, interfaces, and RF review.” Manufacturer sources are the project-local datasheets/integration manuals. The board is not yet proven by an assembled-unit test or a PCBWay impedance/stack-up approval.

## Power budget and temperature

The current design powers the selected GNSS receiver from **V_BAT → TPS63802 → 3V8 → NCV8705 → GNSS_3V3**. Its GNSS current must therefore be charged to the battery path, not directly to the Feather 3V3 regulator. The MAX-M10S [datasheet](../../datasheets/MAX-M10S.pdf) calls out up to 100 mA startup inrush; the ZED-F9P [datasheet](../../datasheets/ZED-F9P.pdf) lists an indicative 130 mA acquisition peak. The active-antenna design allowance is 50 mA, additive to receiver current for preliminary sizing. USB development V_USB draws about 2 mA from USB VBUS through AP2112, not from the GNSS battery regulator.

| Preliminary GNSS-path case | MAX | ZED |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Receiver allowance + active antenna | 100 + 50 = 150 mA | 130 + 50 = 180 mA |
| GNSS_3V3 output power at 3.3 V | 0.495 W | 0.594 W |
| Approximate 3V8-side power at 3.8 V, excluding LDO ground current | 0.570 W | 0.684 W |
| Estimated TPS63802 input current at 3.0 V battery / **assumed** 80% efficiency | 0.238 A | 0.285 A |
| Same at 4.2 V battery / **assumed** 80% efficiency | 0.170 A | 0.204 A |
| Approximate NCV8705 pass-device heat at 3.8→3.3 V | 0.075 W | 0.090 W |

These are planning numbers, not proven worst-case maxima: MAX startup inrush and ZED acquisition peak are different specified conditions; TPS efficiency, antenna current, startup capacitance, regulator ground currents, LED states, Feather 3V3 support loads, and battery-path resistance are not fully bounded here. The MAX datasheet lists 28 µA typical hardware-backup current at 3.3 V; ZED lists 45 µA typical at 3 V. These are small additional V_BAT/MCP1700 loads, but a 3.0 V-input LDO cannot guarantee an exact 3.0 V backup output at the minimum battery voltage; the receiver's 1.65 V backup minimum still leaves headroom. The ZED USB option draws about 2 mA at V_USB from the external 5 V VBUS path. At 80% efficiency the ZED-case TPS heat is about 0.171 W; at 90% it is about 0.076 W. At the listed 116.5 °C/W nominal NCV8705 junction-to-ambient figure, 0.090 W corresponds to roughly 10.5 °C rise, but the actual board/ambient result requires measurement. `V_BAT` and `3V8` include short 0.20 mm track neck-downs; the current rating needs the final copper thickness and thermal/current review. [TI TPS63802](../../datasheets/TPS63802.pdf) §8.3 also requires effective input/output capacitance under DC bias, as recorded in the [passive audit](passive-rating-audit-2026-09-21.md). **Status: open** until a bounded peak/inrush and thermal/copper budget is recorded.

## Power sequencing and back-power paths

The intended battery-on/`GNSS_EN`-on state is coherent: U1 PG drives U2 EN, U2 powers GNSS_3V3, and both receiver VCC pins (or MAX VCC/V_IO) use that rail. V_BACKUP remains on the battery-derived MCP1700 when GNSS_EN is off; this preserves receiver backup state. The ZED USB LDO gets VIN only from USB VBUS and EN from GNSS_3V3; its output powers only V_USB. Thus USB alone is not intended to power the receiver, and USB removal should leave the battery-powered GNSS supply operating.

Several off-state behaviors are still unproven by connectivity alone. The GNSS UART/GPIO nets remain physically connected to the Feather; the firmware must put them in high impedance before GNSS_3V3 shuts down and on boot. MAX/ZED I2C solder links are open in the default UART assembly, which avoids the always-pulled-up sensor bus. With battery on and USB unplugged, AP2112 EN can be at 3.3 V while its VIN is at 0 V. The [AP2112 datasheet](../../datasheets/AP2112.pdf) specifies EN logic levels but does not establish reverse current or back-power behavior for this exact VIN=0/EN=3.3 V state; check this state on a prototype or obtain manufacturer confirmation. With USB present and battery/GNSS off, confirm V_USB stays low and forced USB D+/D− levels cannot exceed the unpowered ZED interface limits. V_BACKUP is not a substitute for powered I/O. **Status: open** pending these off-state tests/confirmation.

## USB-C device interface

Saved J4 `USB4120-03-C` pad mapping agrees with the [GCT drawing](../../datasheets/USB4120_GCT_A3.pdf): A5 and B5 go separately through 5.1 kΩ to GND; A6/B6 join D+ and A7/B7 join D−; A8/B8 are unused; shell pads S1–S4 and GND contacts are grounded. Connector D− enters D8 pin 1 and leaves pin 6 toward R38 and ZED USB_DM pin 39; D+ enters D8 pin 3 and leaves pin 4 toward R39 and USB_DP pin 40. D8 pin 5 follows VBUS, pin 2 GND. R38/R39 are 27 Ω and closer to the ZED than the connector. U4 AP2112 has 1 µF VIN C21 and 1 µF VOUT C24; R41 is a 100 kΩ V_USB pulldown and JP14 is the mutually exclusive no-USB grounding option. VBUS uses 0.30/0.50 mm copper and current DRC finds no clearance violation; sustained VBUS overvoltage protection is not provided.

**Placement update:** C28 was moved to (131.70, 93.13) mm at 90°, about **3.1 mm** center-to-center from ZED V_USB pad 38 at (131.70, 96.20) mm. R41 was moved to (139.20, 91.15) mm; JP14 can remain where it is because neither part is the local high-frequency bypass. All three variant position files were compared with the saved PCB; only the ZED development file needed C28 and R41 updates. A refilled-zone DRC found zero violations and zero unconnected items. Inspect C28's V_USB path and ground return manually in the plotted copper. AP2112 VIN/VOUT capacitors are close to their respective U4 pads (approximately 1.2 mm center-to-center from the nearest capacitor pad). **Status: USB connectivity passes; manual USB layout and off-state tests above remain open.**

## GNSS RF geometry and paths

The MAX and ZED RF paths remain electrically separate, with dedicated U.FL connectors J2/J3, ESD parts D2/D4, chokes L2/L3, and current-limit/bias branches R40/JP4/C16 and R32/JP11/C20. The assembly variants populate only one GNSS subsystem. Both main F.Cu RF traces use 1.05 mm sections with narrower 0.7 mm connector/module transitions. The 0.30/0.40 mm branches to ESD and bias tee create local discontinuities that require RF review. A 0.0707 mm dangling ZED RF copper stub at (129.55, 120.85) mm was removed during this review; the subsequent KiCad DRC reports **0 active violations and 0 unconnected pads**.

**Saved-board zone verification:** The GND zone is defined on both `F.Cu` and `B.Cu`; the PCB stores filled polygons for both layers, and the B.Cu Gerber contains filled regions. A prior claim that B.Cu had no GND zone was incorrect: KiCad's zone API returned the first layer name of the multi-layer zone. The remaining RF task is to inspect *local* continuity beneath both RF traces and the U.FL pad-to-via connections. C28 has its V_USB pad at (131.70, 93.61) mm and GND pad at (131.70, 92.65) mm; the nearest discrete GND via is about 3.2 mm from its GND pad. Verify that C28 joins the F.Cu GND fill without a narrow or indirect return.

The preliminary 1.6 mm FR-4 model in the design document is **not** a PCBWay-controlled stack-up. The saved board has only F.Cu and B.Cu; its KiCad stack-up assumes a 1.51 mm FR-4 core, εr 4.5, 35 µm copper and 10 µm solder mask. The 1.51 mm estimate is simply 1.6 − 2(0.035) − 2(0.010) mm; it assumes the nominal finished board thickness includes both copper and mask layers and is **not** extrapolated from PCBWay's multilayer core/prepreg construction. PCBWay's [published stack-up selector](https://www.pcbway.com/multi-layer-laminated-structure.html) lists four or more layers, so it cannot supply verified production parameters for this two-layer board. PCBWay's [impedance guidance](https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/ordering_parameter_instruction/What_is_Impedance_Control_.html) identifies coplanar traces as controlled-impedance structures and explains the effect of dielectric, Dk, copper and mask. Ask PCBWay for the **order-specific two-layer core/dielectric thickness and material Dk at GNSS frequencies, finished outer copper, solder-mask thickness, and whether they will review/control 50 Ω grounded-coplanar routing**. Supply both Gerbers and a drawing calling out the 1.05 mm nominal RF width and 0.20 mm ground gap, plus the 0.70 mm launch transitions. Recalculate both paths and obtain their written acceptance before release; the public multilayer stack-up alone does not close this check. Each U.FL connector already has a GND via on both sides of its GND pads: the nearest vias are approximately 1.28 mm from the J2 ground-pad centers and 1.38–1.48 mm from the J3 ground-pad centers. Measuring from the connector center had falsely suggested missing return vias; **no mandatory U.FL via addition is established by this coordinate check**. The nearest discrete GND vias at D2 and D4 are about 1.76 mm from the ESD device centers; the L3 choke has no GND via within 2.5 mm of its center. Inspect local B.Cu reference continuity and copper continuity from each U.FL ground pad to its nearby via, connector/ESD return, via-fence spacing, launch geometry, clearances, and bias-branch length in the refilled PCB/Gerbers; add stitching vias only if that review finds a return-path gap. **Stack-up and manual RF-layout items remain open.**

## Antenna compatibility and remaining release work

MAX may use passive L1 with JP4 open or a compatible active L1 antenna with JP4 closed. ZED-F9P-05B needs an active L1/L2 antenna for its intended dual-band use, with JP11 closed by default. The current bias comes from each receiver's VCC_RF through 10 Ω and its 300 mA-rated RF choke; the antenna receives less than VCC_RF under load due to resistor/choke drop. The [antenna candidate table](../../Activity%20Brick%20V2%20Design.md#gnss-antenna-options) lists voltage/current candidates and the ANN-MB2 reference. Production antenna and pigtail ordering codes remain unfrozen until enclosure/ground-plane and RF tests. Active-antenna short behavior is not proved by the receivers' absolute-maximum VCC_RF current numbers; the [passive audit](passive-rating-audit-2026-09-21.md) records the R40/R32 dissipation concern. **Antenna and fault-case item remains open.**
